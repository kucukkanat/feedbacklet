Private by design

Relays carry your traffic but can't read it, link it, or tell who sent it.

  1. Throw-away key

    Every report is signed by a brand-new key generated in the browser and discarded right after sending.

  2. Sealed for you

    The report is encrypted with NIP-44 to your admin public key, baked into the bookmarklet.

  3. Gift wrapped

    The seal is wrapped again with another random key and a fuzzed timestamp (NIP-59).

  4. Only you unwrap

    Your admin inbox decrypts locally in your browser. Status and tags sync encrypted to yourself.
